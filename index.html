📅 YorkU Timetable

Turn your YorkU timetable into a calendar — and manage it throughout the semester.

Paste your class list from Plot My Timetable or add classes manually. Review your schedule, save your classes, and manage them in one place with Today and Week views. See your current and next class, and edit or delete classes whenever your schedule changes.

When you're ready, export your timetable as a .ics file for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and other calendar apps. Term dates, reading weeks, and holidays are handled automatically, so classes don't appear during breaks.

Everything runs in your browser — no login and no data sent anywhere. It works offline and you can install it like an app on your phone or laptop.
